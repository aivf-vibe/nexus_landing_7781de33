Frequently Asked Questions

What are your operating hours?

We're open Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday to Sunday from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time slot.

Do you offer walk-in services?

While we do accept walk-ins when availability permits, we strongly recommend booking an appointment to ensure you receive the time and service you desire, especially during peak hours and weekends.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, and cash payments. We also offer gift cards for special occasions.

Do you use organic products?

Yes! We're committed to using high-quality, organic, and cruelty-free products whenever possible. We carefully select brands that align with our values of sustainability and client wellness.
